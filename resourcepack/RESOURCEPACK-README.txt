NEPTUN SMP — ZYPHIX RESOURCE PACK

Includes five distinct crystal growth stages (stage 1–5), silver-white pixel-art crystal textures, a Zyphix ore texture, and a crystal-covered block texture. The plugin advances crystals through five stages and emits END_ROD, FIREWORK and ENCHANT motes around them.

Install the pack with pack.mcmeta at the ZIP root. Everyone needs the pack to see the custom textures.
